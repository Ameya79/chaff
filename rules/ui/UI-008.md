## Context & Problem
AI landing page generators heavily decorate cards with glassmorphic styling: `backdrop-blur-xl`, `bg-white/10`, thick semi-transparent white borders, and exaggerated purple drop shadows. On mobile hardware and battery-powered laptops, stacked `backdrop-filter` surfaces force continuous GPU compositing and offscreen surface rasterization on every scroll frame, resulting in dropped frames, high battery drain, and muddy, illegible card text.

## Detection Logic
Static AST and CSS scanner identifies `backdrop-blur-*` utilities (`backdrop-blur-md`, `backdrop-blur-lg`, `backdrop-blur-xl`) and `backdrop-filter: blur(...)` declarations applied to repeated card containers, grid children, or nested sections outside of sticky navigation bars.

## Remediation Steps
1. Remove `backdrop-blur-*` utility classes from repeated cards and content panels.
2. Replace translucent glass surfaces with clean, solid or subtle semi-opaque tinted surfaces (such as `bg-stone-900/60` or `bg-zinc-900/80`).
3. Replace blurry translucent borders with crisp 1px solid semantic borders (`border border-stone-800`).
4. Replace heavy diffuse drop shadows (`shadow-2xl`) with tight micro-elevation shadows (`shadow-sm`).

## Verification
Static scanner confirms `backdrop-blur` utilities are strictly confined to top-level sticky navigation bars or modal backdrop overlays. Profile GPU compositor performance during page scrolling to verify sustained 60fps frame rates.
