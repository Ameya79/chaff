## Context & Problem
Untinted `#000000` (pure black) and `#ffffff` (pure white) create artificial, stark visual boundaries that feel harsh and clinical. On OLED displays, pairing pure black with bright white text causes optical halation, where glowing letterforms bleed into dark backgrounds, triggering severe eye strain. High-craft interfaces tint background surfaces with subtle warm or cool undertones to simulate natural physical materials.

## Detection Logic
Static regex and AST scanner searches CSS rules, Tailwind classes, and inline style declarations for raw `#000000`, `#000`, `rgb(0,0,0)`, `bg-black`, `#ffffff`, `#fff`, `rgb(255,255,255)`, and `bg-white` applied to primary background surfaces and cards.

## Remediation Steps
1. In dark mode, replace raw black `#000000` with curated warm or cool tinted dark surfaces (such as `bg-stone-950`, `bg-zinc-950`, or `#0d1117`).
2. In light mode, soften stark white `#ffffff` background fills with warm off-white tones (`bg-stone-50`, `bg-zinc-50`, `#fdfdfc`).
3. Soften high-contrast body text from `#ffffff` down to tinted off-white tokens (`text-stone-100`, `text-zinc-200`).
4. Ensure text contrast against surfaces maintains WCAG AA compliance (4.5:1 minimum).

## Verification
Static scanner verifies zero raw `#000000` or `#ffffff` assignments on background layers in production stylesheets. Color contrast checker validates that tinted foreground-to-background contrast ratios remain above 4.5:1.
