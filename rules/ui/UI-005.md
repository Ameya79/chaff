## Context & Problem
Unconstrained body text containers expand across the full width of widescreen desktop viewports, stretching paragraphs to 120 or more characters per line. Bringhurst's typographic standard establishes 45 to 75 characters as the optimal reading measure. Lines exceeding this boundary cause reader disorientation and fatigue when the eye attempts to sweep back to find the next line start.

## Detection Logic
Headless Playwright audit at 1440px desktop viewport measures computed text line measures on all visible `<p>` elements. The audit samples line length in characters: if the rendered measure exceeds 75 characters per line on multi-line text, a violation is recorded. In static analysis, `<p>` elements without `max-w-prose`, `max-w-xl`, or explicit column width constraints inside full-width sections are flagged.

## Remediation Steps
1. Apply `max-w-prose` (approx 65 characters) or `max-w-xl` to long-form paragraph wrappers.
2. Pair the constrained line measure with proportional line spacing (`leading-relaxed` or line-height: 1.625).
3. In multi-column editorial layouts, break wide paragraphs into multi-column CSS grids or balanced side-by-side callouts.
4. Ensure line measures on mobile devices remain comfortably above 35 characters.

## Verification
Playwright rendered test at 1440px viewport validates line measure across all body paragraph elements is less than or equal to 75 characters per line.
